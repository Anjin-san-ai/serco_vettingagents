Risk register

Taken verbatim from your business cases

These are not our risks about your process — they are the risks your own business cases record. Each one became a design constraint on a specific agent, which is the test of whether a solution actually read the documents.

Stated riskSourceWhich agent answers it, and how
“Managers sending invalid documents (currently the team will filter unrequired docs out i.e. bank statements)” BC 1 [WS p11] A2 classifies and rejects out-of-scope documents before they reach SAP; A1 stops them being submitted at all
“Managers sending multiple docs in a combined PDF (needs to be split prior to upload)” BC 1 [WS p11] A2 splits a combined PDF into discrete classified documents
“Non-standard documents? Escalation process required.” BC 2 [WS p12] A2 emits a per-field confidence score; anything below threshold escalates to a human rather than being written to SAP
“Conditions MUST be met. If the role sits within ER, it MUST NOT be sent via ticket to ER team as it will be visible to all ER team. Also condition for VIVO to be explored.” BC 3 [WS p13] A10 under a central routing guard in the Assurance plane. VIVO is flagged as unresolved, not assumed
“Reliance on digital verification may create a perception that physical document validation is no longer required” Brainstorm ref 1 [WS p7] A3 emits an assurance level, never a bare pass, and records what was and was not verifiable
“Risk of acknowledgement becoming a ‘click-through’ exercise without improving understanding or compliance” Brainstorm ref 2 [WS p7] A1 is a submission gate, not a checkbox — it blocks rather than warns
“The diverse and complex nature of vetting requirements will increase challenges to achieve a standardised output” Brainstorm ref 3 [WS p7] A1 holds the case when circumstances fall outside the modelled rule set instead of guessing a document list
“Accuracy may vary depending on document quality, format, and document type” Brainstorm ref 4 [WS p7] Wave 0 exists for exactly this: A2 and A4 run in shadow mode and are measured per document type before anything is automated

Open questions

Seven things we need from Serco

  1. The rework figure. Is avoidable RTW rework 11.5 hrs/month or ~19.1? Both appear in the deck.
  2. The VIVO condition on follow-on routing is “to be explored” in business case 3. What is the rule?
  3. ER self-referral. How is “the role sits within ER” determined at audit time — SAP org unit, cost centre, something else? The guard depends on it.
  4. Specialist Vetting sign-off. What has to happen for that map to lose its “DRAFT — DON'T AUTOMATE” status?
  5. Volumes and AHT for Direct Vetting, Specialist Vetting and AFRS. The failure rates are published; the volumes are not, so no benefit can responsibly be claimed.
  6. AFRS VDI. Is the no-copy-paste, no-Access-Screening-link constraint a fixed platform boundary or configuration? It decides whether A5 can reach AFRS at all.
  7. Access Screening integration. Does AS expose an API or webhook for link completion, or is polling genuinely the only route? A6's design depends on the answer.
