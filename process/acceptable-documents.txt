Right to Work & Screening/Vetting Checks
Acceptable Document Guide for Hiring Managers
Purpose: This guide explains what Right to Work (RTW) evidence and additional screening/vetting ID documents hiring managers need
to collect, how to check/certify them, and when to escalate to Vetting & Onboarding (VOB).
Manager quick actions before offer approval
• Confirm the candidate will be living in the UK for the UK role, including remote-working roles.
• Collect the correct RTW evidence based on nationality/status and upload it to the external offer form.
• For non-UK/Irish candidates, obtain the GOV.UK share code/eVisa and date of birth; share codes expire after 90 days.
• For UK/Irish valid passports, use IDVT where available or complete a physical original-document check.
• Reject and re-request any unclear, cropped, expired, incomplete or uncertified evidence.
• Where names differ, obtain supporting evidence such as marriage certificate, civil partnership certificate, divorce decree or deed poll.
1. Document quality standards - reject and re-request if not met
Standard
What hiring managers must check
Full image
All four corners must be visible. Do not accept cropped images, only the photo page of a passport, or missing
pages that show restrictions/endorsements.
Clear and legible
No glare, blur, faded dates or obscured name, date of birth, passport number, expiry date, visa status or
address.
Separate evidence
Each ID document must be uploaded separately and show full verification/certification details on the relevant
page.
Certification date
Document certification should normally be within 6 months. A GOV.UK share code/eVisa check must be current;
share codes expire after 90 days.
Name accuracy
The name spelling on the PF/offer form must match the passport or primary ID. Middle name is not essential on
the PF form, but name discrepancies must be explained and evidenced.
eVisa legal name
The online immigration status/eVisa must show the candidate’s current legal name. Escalate to VOB if the
candidate cannot amend it.
Photo likeness
The checker must confirm that the photograph on the document, IDVT report or GOV.UK result matches the
person presenting for work.
Audit trail
Use the exact certification wording, include the checker’s full name and date, and retain/upload the full PDF
where applicable.

3. How to check and certify documents
Check type
Process and required certification
IDVT - UK/Irish valid passports
Request via MyHR Portal > Ask MyHR > Vetting & Onboarding > Remote ID Checks. Provide the candidate’s
full name and email. VOB emails the candidate, receives the IDVT outcome, and provides the PDF report.
The hiring manager/requestor must check the photographs for likeness.
GOV.UK share code/eVisa
Candidate provides a share code and date of birth. run the check on GOV.UK, download the full PDF, confirm
the photo matches the candidate, and certify: “Confirmation photo matches the candidate - [name], [date]”.
Physical original-document check
Obtain the original document, check it in the presence of the holder, copy/scan the full document and certify:
“Check of ORIGINAL document performed on [date] by [full name]” or “I certify this is a true copy of the
original - [full name], [date]”.
Video check for supporting/vetting
ID
Where permitted, obtain a scan/photo in advance, check the document and photo during a live video call, and
certify: “Live video check of document undertaken on [date] by [full name]”. Original documents may still need
to be presented before employment/Day 1 depending on the route.
Follow-up RTW checks
For time-limited permission, VOB will contact the line manager around 3 months before expiry. The employee
must make any in-time application and provide updated evidence before the expiry/follow-up deadline.
Visas in expired passports
If a visa is attached to an expired passport, ask the candidate to provide an up-to-date share code/eVisa or
document. Do not approve the offer until VOB has verified the status.
TUPE transferring employees
RTW checks are required for all employees transferring into Serco and must be completed within 40 days of
the transfer date, including absent employees.
4. Acceptable ID documents for screening/vetting checks
This section is for additional screening/vetting checks only - not as a substitute for Right to Work checks. If the document is not
listed, ask the candidate for an acceptable replacement or escalate to VOB. Serco Justice and Immigration contracts may have bespoke
requirements.
Route
Required combination
Route 1 - use wherever possible
1 document from Group 1 + 2 further documents from Group 1, 2a or 2b. At least one document should
confirm current address and the combination must confirm name and date of birth.
Route 2 - only if no Group 1 document
1 document from Group 2a + 2 further documents from Group 2a or 2b. At least one document should
confirm current address. VOB/DBS may also require external ID verification.
Route 3 - exception only
Birth certificate issued more than 12 months after birth + 1 document from Group 2a + 3 further
documents from Group 2a or 2b. At least one document should confirm current address. Escalate
before using Route 3.
